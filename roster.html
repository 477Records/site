    Bec Lauder and the Noise are a New York glam-punk trio: Bec Lauder on vocals and guitar, Maggie Bishop on drums and Soph Shreds on guitar. Their 477 releases include “Hard To Get”, “More Than Chemical” and the EP Before Everything Changes.
