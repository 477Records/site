Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. This website has no accounts, forms, mailing list, analytics or advertising tracking, and it does not set cookies.

Our host

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Links to other services

Links to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and Instagram take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Questions

About

477 Records is a New York-based independent record label.

Founded by Two Feet, a platinum-and-gold-certified musician and producer, in 2024, 477 Records grew out of his experience as an independent artist.

We find, sign, and develop independent artists and help them develop their sound. We offer the reach and services of a major label while staying independent.
